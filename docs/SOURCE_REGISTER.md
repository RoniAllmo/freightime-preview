## Explicitly not relied upon as primary authority

Several commercial law-firm and accessibility-vendor articles surfaced in
search results (e.g. accessibility-implementation vendor sites) were
**read for orientation only and are not cited as authority** in the legal
drafts, per this task's instruction not to rely solely on commercial
articles. No text was copied from any of them; the drafts in this repo are
original wording informed by the principles found in the official sources
above.

## Verification method and limitations

- Searches were run through the WebSearch tool available in this session
  on 2026-08-16. Results are general web search, not a direct gov.il
  crawl — several results point to `nevo.co.il` (Israel's standard public
  legislation-database mirror) and `knesset.gov.il` / `mevaker.gov.il`
  rather than pages hosted directly under `gov.il`. This is normal for
  Israeli legislation research (the legislation itself is not always
  mirrored on a `gov.il` domain) but is recorded here explicitly so a
  professional reviewer can re-verify against primary `gov.il` /
  `justice.gov.il` sources before publication.
- No source in this register should be read as a legal opinion. Every row
  requires professional legal (and, for accessibility rows, professional
  accessibility) verification before any compliance claim is made public.
- This register was not exhaustive — a full legal audit would also
  consult the Privacy Protection Authority's own guidance documents, the
  Equal Rights Commission's official accessibility guidance, and the
  Ministry of Communications where applicable, using authenticated /
  primary gov.il sources rather than general web search.
